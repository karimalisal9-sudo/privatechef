About the chef

From Ikaria to the Alps.

I grew up in my family's restaurant on the Greek island of Ikaria, which has been cooking for guests for fifty years. Since then I have worked as a private chef in luxury villas in Mykonos, in five-star resorts and in fine-dining kitchens.

This winter I am based in the Salzburger Land. Book me for a dinner party, a family celebration or a full week of breakfasts and dinners in your chalet.

Aufgewachsen im Familienrestaurant auf Ikaria, Privatkoch in Luxusvillen auf Mykonos, Fünf-Sterne-Resorts und Fine Dining.

About the chef

From Ikaria to the Alps.

Questions

Good to know.

What is included in the price?

Menu planning, shopping for fresh ingredients, cooking in your kitchen, serving every course and cleaning the kitchen afterwards. You don't need to buy or prepare anything.

Are drinks included?

No. You choose your own wine and drinks. I'm happy to suggest pairings for your menu.

How many guests can you cook for?

From 2 to 20 guests, as a seated dinner or a sharing-style feast.

Can you handle allergies and special diets?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free and allergies are planned into the menu from the start. Just tell me when you send your request.

Do I need a big kitchen?

No. A normal home or apartment kitchen with an oven and a stove is enough. I bring the special tools I need.

Where do you cook?

In the city of Salzburg and the Salzburger Land: homes, holiday apartments and chalets. Ask me about other places nearby.

How far ahead should I book?

One to two weeks is ideal. For Christmas and New Year's Eve, book as early as possible because dates are limited. Last-minute requests are welcome, just ask.

How do I pay?

A deposit confirms your date. The rest is paid after the dinner by bank transfer or cash.

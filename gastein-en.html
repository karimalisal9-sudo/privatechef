About the chef

From Ikaria to the Gastein valley.

I grew up in my family's restaurant on the Greek island of Ikaria, which has been feeding guests for fifty years. Since then I have cooked as a private chef in luxury villas in Mykonos, in five-star resorts and in fine-dining kitchens.

This winter I live and cook in Bad Gastein. I know the valley, the local suppliers, and what people want to eat after a long day on the slopes.
